# Notice: data derived from Odoo's demo data (LGPL-3)

Part of the dataset in `data/` (and the rule in `construct.py` that writes it) is derived from the
demo and base data of Odoo's Manufacturing module, `addons/mrp` (files `data/mrp_demo.xml` and
`data/mrp_data.xml`) in odoo/odoo, branch 20.0, commit `873d27cfffa16075ce44dbc5ce7dc6a99198b413`.
Odoo Community is licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License version 3 (LGPL-3):
https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/20.0/LICENSE

Taken from it: the work centre names Assembly 1, Assembly 2 and Drill 1; the calendar
"Work Center 40 hours/week" (Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 16:00); the product names Table, Desk,
Table Top, Drawer, Drawer Case Black, Wood Panel and Plastic Laminate; and the operation names
Assembly, Long time assembly, Testing and Packing.

These derived parts are distributed under the LGPL-3. The other work centres, the routings,
quantities, dates, deadlines and floor state are generated by `construct.py`.

The Docker image built by `rerun.sh` starts from the official `odoo` image on Docker Hub (pinned
by digest in `Dockerfile`) and adds PostgreSQL 16 from apt.postgresql.org and libfaketime 0.9.13
(GPL-2.0, https://github.com/wolfcw/libfaketime). Nothing from those is included in this directory.

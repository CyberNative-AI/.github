    Run an incident-learning session with your team

    Put this on the projector. The group makes the engineers' real decisions, sees what a simplified model does with them, then compares them with the official record. Nothing is saved or sent; reloading clears the session.

      5. 23–25Print or save the one-page summary; note actions.
